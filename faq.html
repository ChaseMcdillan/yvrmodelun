Frequently asked questions

Who can attend? +

YVRMUN is designed as a youth Model United Nations conference for high school students in Metro Vancouver. Exact eligibility range will be published here once registration policy is finalized.

Do I need previous MUN experience? +

Not necessarily. Committee descriptions indicate experience levels, and staff prepare conference materials to help delegates get up to speed — whether it's your first committee or your tenth.

When and where is Session II? +

Session II is planned for April 2027 in Richmond, British Columbia. Exact dates and venue will be announced here once confirmed.

Can I apply for staff? +

Yes. Use the staff application. Open positions include Flex Staff, Assistant Director, Chair, and Director.

What is the Press Corps? +

The Press Corps is a non-voting newsroom inside the conference. It covers debate, interviews delegates, publishes stories, and shapes how the conference is remembered.

Is the schedule final? +

No. The schedule is a working outline and will be replaced with the official timetable once conference logistics are finalized.

05 / THE WEEKEND

One day. One room.

Working outline only. Exact times and venue details will be updated here once the conference schedule is finalized.

Conference schedule

  1. 8:00 – 8:15

    Registration

    Badges, room assignments, last-minute nerves, and the first coffee.

  2. 8:20 – 8:45

    Opening Ceremony

    Welcome, keynote, committee introductions, and the official call to order.

  3. 9:00 – 10:30

    Committee Session I

    Speakers' lists, caucuses, first alliances, and the beginning of the paper trail.

  4. 10:30 – 10:40

    Break

  5. 10:45 – 12:15

    Committee First Iteration

    Working papers take shape. Alliances harden. The room starts to move.

  6. 12:15 – 1:00

    Lunch

    The formal debate stops. The actual diplomacy probably does not.

  7. 1:00 – 2:30

    Committee First IterationI

    Working papers become draft resolutions. Crisis rooms start moving faster.

  8. 2:30 – 2:40

    Break

  9. 2:45 – 4:15

    Committee Session IV

    Amendments fly. Coalitions shift. The consequences of earlier decisions arrive.

  10. 4:15 – 4:25

    Break

  11. 4:30 – 6:00

    Committee Session V / Crisis

    Crisis directives, breaking updates, and the moments delegates remember.

  12. 6:00 – 6:10

    Break

  13. 6:15 – 6:45

    Committee Session VI / FunMUN

    Looser rules, sharper wit, and a final round of chaos before awards.

  14. 6:45 – 7:30

    Closing Ceremony

    Awards, acknowledgements, final remarks, and the handoff to First IterationI.

01 / WHY THIS EXISTS

MUN should feel alive.

A good committee is not a worksheet with microphones. It is a room full of people trying to move one another. The best moments are improvised, inconvenient, funny, serious, and impossible to predict from the background guide.

YVRMUN is designed around that feeling. Small details matter. The topic matters. The dais matters. The person sitting across from you matters. We want delegates to leave remembering an argument, not just a score.

02 / THE SETTING

07 / QUESTIONS

You ask. We answer.

A few practical things before you commit a weekend to diplomacy.

Who can attend?

YVRMUN is designed as a youth Model United Nations conference. Publish your actual eligibility range here once registration policy is finalized.

Do I need experience?

Not necessarily. Committee descriptions can indicate experience levels, and staff can use conference materials to help delegates prepare.

When and where is Session II?

YVRMUN Session II is planned for April 2027 in Richmond, British Columbia. Publish exact dates and venue here once confirmed.

Can I apply for staff?

Yes. Use the staff application above. The current template supports Chair, Crisis Staff, Logistics, and Press positions.

What is the Press Corps?

The Press Corps is a non-voting newsroom inside the conference. It can cover debate, interview delegates, publish stories, and shape how the conference is remembered.

Is the schedule final?

No. The schedule shown is a working outline and should be replaced with the official timetable when the conference logistics are finalized.

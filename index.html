04 / Good to know

A few first questions.

What is Model United Nations?

Model UN is an educational simulation of international diplomacy. Participants represent countries or other stakeholders, discuss real-world issues, and work together on proposed solutions.

Do I need Model UN experience?

No prior experience is needed to learn the basics of Model UN. YVRMUN participation requirements and any preparation resources will be included with the conference announcement.

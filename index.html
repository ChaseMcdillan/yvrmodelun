Letter from the Secretary-General

A word from Bruce.

Dear teachers, staff, and prospective delegates,

It is an incredible honour to welcome you to the first iteration of Youth Visionary Reformers Model United Nations — happening April 17, 2027, in beautiful Richmond, British Columbia. YVRMUN is a unique experience welcoming over 80 delegates to a memorable day of Model United Nations.

The idea of YVRMUN was created in May of 2026, when a few local Model UN participants expressed the lack of local and accessible conferences here in Richmond. The idea grew, and eventually became known as YVRMUN. The vision of YVRMUN is to spread geopolitical knowledge and to provide equity among all delegates and staff members in the MUN space.

This year, YVRMUN is excited to introduce three distinct committees — stretching from a general assembly, to a specialized agency, and also a crisis committee — offering unique experiences for all levels of delegates. Whether this is your first Model United Nations conference or your last in your high school MUN career, YVRMUN is perfected for each delegate and will allow all to challenge their knowledge, sharpen their debate skills, and collaborate to face pressing issues.

Although Model United Nations is a highly diplomatic sport, it is also a space to create memories and friendships that will last a lifetime. I encourage all delegates to make new friendships and help those who might be new to Model United Nations.

Most importantly: YVRMUN does not tolerate any form of harassment, bullying, or assault. Any delegates found participating in harassment, bullying, or assault may be removed from the conference without refund and blacklisted from future YVRMUN iterations.

Lastly, if you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at yvrmun26@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as possible. On behalf of the Secretary-General and staff team, we hope to see you at the first iteration of YVRMUN on April 17, 2027, and will strive to create a memorable experience.

Sincerely,
Bruce Sui

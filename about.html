01 / WHY THIS EXISTS

MUN should feel alive.

A good committee is not a worksheet with microphones. It is a room full of people trying to move one another.

The mission

The best moments in MUN are improvised, inconvenient, funny, serious, and impossible to predict from the background guide. YVRMUN is designed around that feeling. Small details matter. The topic matters. The dais matters. The person sitting across from you matters.

We want delegates to leave remembering an argument, not just a score.

02 / THE SETTING

The room is somewhere.

Richmond is close enough to feel local and large enough to make a weekend conference feel like an event. YVRMUN takes over the city for two days of diplomacy, strategy, press, and controlled chaos.
